# BROAD dataset attribution

The six HDF5 recordings are unmodified copies from the Berlin Robust Orientation Estimation Assessment Dataset by Daniel Laidig, Marco Caruso, Andrea Cereatti, and Thomas Seel. Kai Brands recorded the data, as credited in the original files. The files are distributed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
